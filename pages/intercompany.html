New Stock Transfer / Supply
Quick Workflow Guide: • Dispatching an Order for a Co-Marketing Partner or Dealer? You do not need to enter it here! Go directly to Sales Orders → Prepare Dispatch. You can choose whether it delivers directly to the customer or stocks into the partner godown—in 1 single step with zero duplicate entry! • Moving unsold bulk stock between internal Warehouses? Use this form! You do not need to manage batch numbers; factory inventory is tracked and allocated automatically.

Step 1: Supplying & Receiving Entities

Choose the company providing the stock and the company receiving it, then select their warehouses.

Step 2: Products & Line Items

Select products, packaging sizes, and quantities to transfer. Available warehouse inventory is allocated automatically.

Auto-Fill from Sales Order 1-Click Import
Select an existing sales order to automatically load all products, packaging sizes, quantities, and rates without typing!

Step 3: Commercial Terms & Transport Details

Supply pricing terms, target dispatch date, and transport vehicle details.

Step 4: Review & Confirm

Verify the transfer details before creating the record.
